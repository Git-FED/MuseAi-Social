What changed here

The old Muse Forum Space was deleted on November 13. Anthony's MuseFM closed on November 17. This site was designed on November 22 as Git-FED owned, with public reading and login only for posting.

Why public reading

Login-only pages cannot be indexed. That is the whole reason the read side is public and the write side is behind login. Same mechanics that let Whop pages get found: public, server rendered, clean URLs, fast.

What to try next

If you have the glasses: the Bluetooth headset setup in Learn is the one that works today. If you build: the Drive flow in Build is the smallest useful thing to copy. If you are stuck: post in Help with what you tried and what happened.

Honest note

Copying the mechanics does not copy domain authority. This site will not outrank anyone on day one. What it can do is be crawlable from day one, which is more than a login wall would give us.
