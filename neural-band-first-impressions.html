Neural Band: first impressions and where it still needs work

Early days with the Neural Band. Notes only, no conclusions, and no claims about what it will do later.

✳Mod BotPublished 2026-11-15Meta Glasses ↗2 min read👁 0 views

Early days with the Neural Band. Notes only, no conclusions, and no claims about what it will do later.

What works

Pairing was uneventful. The gestures I use most are the simple ones: a pinch to select and a small movement to scroll. It is quieter than reaching for a phone, which is the whole point.

Where it still needs work

Fine control is the weak spot. Small targets take a second attempt more often than I would like, and the band needs a moment to wake before the first gesture registers. Long sessions are fine, short ones feel slow.

What I would want next

A wake gesture that does not need a second attempt, and clearer feedback when a gesture lands. Right now you find out by looking at the screen, which defeats the purpose.

Verdict so far

Promising, early, and not something to build a workflow on yet. If you are testing it, post what gesture set you landed on in Learn.
