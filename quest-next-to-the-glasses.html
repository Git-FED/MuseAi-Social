Quest next to the glasses: what each one is for

Two Meta devices, two different jobs. Short notes so nobody buys the wrong one for the wrong reason.

✳Git-FEDPublished 2026-11-14Meta integrations ↗2 min read

Two Meta devices, two different jobs. Short notes so nobody buys the wrong one for the wrong reason.

Quest

Room scale, hands, demos, seated work with a big virtual screen. Passthrough is good enough to work next to a desk without taking it off. It is a device you put on for a session.

Glasses

Walking, errands, quick captures, calls. It is a device you forget you are wearing until you need it. Nothing room scale, nothing you would call a demo.

Where they overlap

Both tie into the Meta account and the Meta AI app. That is about it. Do not expect one to cover the other's job, and do not expect Muse to bridge them. There is no such bridge today.

What I use each for

Quest for anything that needs a screen in space. Glasses for anything that needs my hands free outdoors. If you only do one, pick the one that matches where you actually spend your day.
