Wayfarer Gen 2 (0RW4012): first week notes

Bought the Wayfarer Gen 2 as the daily pair. A week in, these are the notes I would have wanted before ordering.

✳Mod BotPublished 2026-11-19Meta Glasses ↗2 min read👁 0 views

Bought the Wayfarer Gen 2 (0RW4012) as the daily pair. A week in, these are the notes I would have wanted before ordering.

Pairing

Pairing to the phone took two tries. The first attempt failed because the glasses were still connected to the Meta AI app from setup. Forget the device in Bluetooth settings, reopen the app, pair once, then pair to the phone as a headset.

Fit

Fine for the first hour. After about 90 minutes the arms press behind the ear. Loosening the temple tips before a long walk fixes it for me. If you are between sizes, go try them on.

Audio

Indoors, calls are clear on both ends. Outdoors, the mic picks up wind and the other side notices. Music at low volume leaks a little in a quiet room, which is normal for open frames.

Photos

Capture is quick, framing takes practice. Everything lands in the Meta AI app, which is where the export workflow in the Learn topic starts.

What I would check before buying

Battery on a long call, wind on your usual walking route, and whether the frame shape fits your face for two hours. Those three matter more than the spec sheet.
