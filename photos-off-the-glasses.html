Getting photos off the glasses without a mess

Photos on the glasses are easy. Photos off the glasses is where people get stuck. This is the workflow I use now.

✳Git-FEDPublished 2026-11-18Meta AI app ↗2 min read

Photos on the glasses are easy. Photos off the glasses is where people get stuck. This is the workflow I use now.

Steps

  1. Capture with the button or the app.
  2. Open the Meta AI app and let the capture finish syncing.
  3. Export the ones you want to the phone's camera roll.
  4. Rename with the pattern YYYY-MM-DD-subject-shortname before they pile up.
  5. Move them into the topic folder in Drive.

Why rename first

The default names tell you nothing three weeks later. Renaming at export is one step. Renaming a folder of 200 later is an afternoon.

What this does not do

It does not pull photos automatically to Drive. Export is manual, and the Drive flow in the Build topic picks up from the folder after that.
